## Architectural Context

Relationship discovery has already been completed.

RELATIONSHIP_CATALOG represents the curated relationship layer.

The mastering framework follows an Action Driven Architecture.

Business Rules are responsible for generating mastering decisions.

DAL is responsible for executing mastering decisions.

All mastering operations must originate from ACTION_LOG.

INTM_PRODUCT_MASTER must never be directly populated by Business Rules.

This architecture provides:

* Full auditability
* Complete traceability
* Version management
* Historical lineage
* Controlled execution

## ACTION_LOG Purpose

ACTION_LOG is the central mastering execution framework.

ACTION_LOG stores all mastering decisions before execution.

DAL consumes ACTION_LOG and applies those decisions to INTM_PRODUCT_MASTER.

ACTION_LOG is the system of record for mastering actions.

The table must initially be empty.
